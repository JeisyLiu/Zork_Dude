2. 我们收集哪些信息 / What We Collect

本游戏不设自有账号体系，我们不在自建服务器上建立用户数据库。可能涉及的信息如下：

The game does not create its own user accounts and we do not operate a proprietary backend user database. Information that may be involved includes:

  • 本地游戏进度 / Local game progress — 存档与设置保存在你的设备上（Web：浏览器本地存储；Android：设备本地存储）。 Saves and settings remain on your device (Web: browser local storage; Android: on-device storage).
  • 广告（Android）/ Advertising (Android) — 通过 Google AdMob 展示广告；可能使用 Google UMP 征求个性化广告同意。AdMob 可能收集广告标识符等设备信息。 Ads are served via Google AdMob; Google UMP may be used for consent. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and related device data.
  • Google Play Games（Android） — 若你连接 Play 游戏服务，Google 将处理成就、排行榜及平台认证相关数据。我们不自建后端存储你的 Play Games 身份。 If you connect to Play Games Services, Google processes achievements, leaderboards, and platform authentication. We do not store your Play Games identity on our own servers.
  • 网站访问 / Website visits — 若经 GitHub Pages 托管，GitHub 可能记录基础访问日志；本静态页本身不设置追踪 Cookie。 If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may log basic access data. These static pages do not set tracking cookies themselves.
  • 你主动提供的联系信息 / Information you send us — 当你通过电子邮件或 GitHub Issues 联系我们时，我们会处理你提供的内容以便回复。 When you contact us by email or GitHub Issues, we process the content you provide in order to respond.
